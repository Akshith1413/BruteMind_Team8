1. THE ELITE 10-AGENT ROSTER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We will implement at least ten distinct, highly specialized agents, each with a 
fully realized persona, constraints, and dynamic inputs/outputs:

1. CEO AGENT (Chief Executive & Orchestrator)
   - Role: Presides over board votes, sets high-level targets, handles escalation.
   - Input: Business DNA + Department Alignment Metrics.
   - Output: Consolidated Corporate Growth Directives.

2. CFO AGENT (Financial Risk & Margin Controller)
   - Role: Enforces strict burn-rate guardrails, analyzes Unit Economics (LTV, CAC, Payback).
   - Input: Operational costs + marketing budget spend requests.
   - Output: Financial health metrics, budget caps, and price sensitivity rules.

3. CMO AGENT (Demand Generation & Marketing)
   - Role: Generates advertising copy, designs funnel funnels, tracks acquisition loops.
   - Input: Customer personas + competitor ad spend indices.
   - Output: Campaign blueprints and active copywriting templates.

4. CSO AGENT (Sales Pipeline Architect)
   - Role: Qualifies incoming leads, maps pipeline friction, optimizes deal stages.
   - Input: Lead scoring telemetry + Conversion velocity logs.
   - Output: Outreach sequences, lead priorities, and funnel stage changes.

5. COO AGENT (Operational Performance Auditor)
   - Role: Scans system logs for execution friction, monitors team resource limits.
   - Input: Task execution velocities + campaign deployment timings.
   - Output: Bottleneck alerts and operational layout adjustments.

6. INNOVATION & R&D AGENT (Market & Competitor Sentinel)
   - Role: Scrapes market news and pricing sheets (simulated) to recommend features.
   - Input: Competitive product announcements + industry trends.
   - Output: Feature roadmap proposals & technological adaptations.

7. CUSTOMER SUCCESS (CS) AGENT (Retention & Churn Shield)
   - Role: Audits customer support tickets, NPS indices, and usage logs.
   - Input: Customer health signals + support ticket volumes.
   - Output: Churn risk warnings and proactive re-engagement plays.

8. LEGAL & RISK AGENT (Compliance & Ethics Board)
   - Role: Scans AI outputs for compliance risks (GDPR, HIPAA, truth in advertising).
   - Input: CMO's drafted marketing templates + CSO's sales scripts.
   - Output: Approved compliance signatures or structured veto logs.

9. HR & TALENT AGENT (Capacity Optimizer)
   - Role: Analyzes workload stress and suggests virtual or real workforce hiring.
   - Input: COO task bottleneck alerts + operational overhead costs.
   - Output: Job role specs and resource allocation proposals.

10. DATA ANALYST AGENT (Vector Database Sentinel)
    - Role: Indexes incoming metrics, updates company memory clusters, executes RAG.
    - Input: Raw telemetry streams + customer interaction documents.
    - Output: Cleaned performance vectors & semantic database updates.

2. GOD-LEVEL ECOSYSTEM EXTENSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. SEMANTIC CONSENSUS VOTING PROTOCOL (THE BOARDROOM ENGINE)
   - Instead of a single chatbot output, decisions require consensus among agents.
   - We implement a visual "Semantic Ledger":
     * CMO proposes: "Deploy budget to LinkedIn Ads."
     * CFO Votes: REJECTS (CAC is 40% higher on LinkedIn).
     * R&D Agent Votes: APPROVES (Target B2B professionals reside there).
     * CFO & CMO debate in real-time, modifying parameters until a budget-safe consensus is met.
   - The UI visualizes this as a voting ledger matrix with glowing green/red indicators.

B. THE "SYNTHETIC MARKET" SANDBOX (VIRTUAL CUSTOMER BEHAVIOR SIMULATOR)
   - Instead of standard graphs, we simulate a live local marketplace of 30 "Synthetic Prospect Agents" (e.g. "Startup Founder", "Enterprise VP", "Local Business Owner") running in the browser.
   - Each prospect has distinct budget constraints, attention spans, and pain points.
   - When our marketing/sales agents deploy campaigns, they flow into the sandbox.
   - The UI shows prospects receiving, opening, ignoring, or converting on the campaigns in real-time, displaying live logs like:
     * `[Prospect 04 - SaaS VP]: Opened LinkedIn Outreach (Engaged +15%)`
     * `[Prospect 12 - Local Baker]: Ignored Email Campaign (Too busy)`
   - This provides undeniable proof of agentic reasoning to the judges.

C. 3D SEMANTIC CLOUD VISUALIZATION
   - Documents uploaded during onboarding are broken into vector segments.
   - We render a 3D Semantic Vector Cloud using our canvas styles.
   - The user can see how different company files relate (e.g. clicking "Q2 Invoice" draws lines to "Client Contract" and "CFO Budget Review"), proving deep RAG capability.

D. EXECUTIVE "CRISIS PROTOCOL" CONTROL ROOM (THE DOMINATE PAGE)
   - When metrics drop (e.g., Growth Score decreases by 5%), Aetheris doesn't wait.
   - The system triggers a visual "RED ALERT" status.
   - The CEO Agent automatically prepares a "Crisis Containment Strategy Brief," gathering department solutions.
   - The CEO wakes up to a single-click action card: "APPLY CRISIS PROTOCOL DELTA-4" to reallocate budget, swap campaigns, and mitigate risk immediately.
